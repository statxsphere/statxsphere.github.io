My story, in halves

Hi there, and welcome.

Kick-off2018 · sports

My data journey started with sports. I've always been drawn to the moments of magic that happen in a stadium, and by college I knew I wanted to work in the industry behind them. That led to my first internship in 2018, where I learned how sports businesses actually run and, more lastingly, got hooked on data science.

First half2019–21 · the startup

With minimal experience, maximal ambition and a nudge from my MBA, I co-founded a sports analytics firm in 2019 to fill gaps I saw in Indian sports analytics. We were bootstrapped, signed academies across Bengaluru, and quietly hoped to help India reach a FIFA World Cup one day. Then the pandemic arrived, and it is hard to analyse sports when no sports are being played. We shut down. It was a tough stretch, and also the one where I grew the most technically.

Second half2021–24 · climate tech

The pandemic also changed what I wanted to work on. Climate change felt like the next large, slow-moving risk, and I wanted my skills pointed at it. From 2021 I moved deliberately into climate tech, focusing on solar energy and carbon-emissions monitoring, where my interests and my potential impact overlap the most.

Extra time2024– · maps

In August 2024 I joined Chimnie, a UK property-data company, and went from analysing footballers to mapping roofs. I led Polygons, a model that reads aerial photos and LiDAR height data to outline every building in Great Britain: 24.2 million of them, each linked to its addresses.

Full time?not yet

Today I'm looking to own bigger problems end to end: the data, the model, and the people who rely on the result. If you're working on energy, mapping or climate and could use that, I'd be glad to talk. Cheers!
